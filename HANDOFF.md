### What v0.7.7 built that the next version meets

- **The switches, and how each gives v0.7.6 back.** `?towns77=0` (node `FS_TOWNS77=0`; `TOWNS77` and `towns77Switch()` in `src/world/cities.js`, re-exported by `centres.js`): the centres' worker is asked for no courtyards and builds exactly v0.7.6's buffers from an `FSB2` file, the town's shader text is v0.7.6's, the terrain takes no towns' uniform. `?fields77=0` (node `FS_FIELDS77=0`; `FIELDS77` in `src/world/ground.js`): `euGroundGlsl()` returns v0.7.6's text and `terrain.js` binds no zone texture. `?suburbs77=0` (node `FS_SUBURBS77=0`; `SUBURBS77 = TOWNS77 && suburbs77Switch()`): the suburbs alone off, with the courtyards, the edge blend and the river rule left on, and v0.7.6's courtyard rule for the blocks; on by default (decisions 17, 19, 22 and 23). Each is made as `apron.js`'s `lookSwitch()` is, and proved by SHA-256 hashes taken from the frozen v0.7.6 (`tests/v077-towns-courtyards.test.mjs`, `v077-towns-blend.test.mjs`, `v077-country-fields.test.mjs`); East Asia's shader texts and centres are the same with them on or off, pinned. v0.7.8's look changes go behind switches of their own the same way.
- **`FSB2`, the centres' format,** and who reads it: `FSB1`'s bytes after the magic unchanged, then u32 holeCount, u32 holeVertexCount, 8-byte records (u32 record, u16 nVerts, u16 0) and the clockwise i16 vertices of each courtyard. `tools/bake_region_buildings.py` writes it with `FS_CENTRES_HOLES=1` (`hole_rings()`: each courtyard clipped 0.5 m inside the outer ring, simplified to about 1 m, under 60 m² or narrower than 6 m dropped); `src/world/centres-worker.js` reads it (`MAGIC2`, `parseHoles()`, `triangulateHoles()` with three's earcut, which falls back to the closed roof when its area check fails); `tests/v076-eu-centres.test.mjs` holds an `FSB2` file by its outer part's SHA-256 (`OUTER77`). Europe's thirty-four centres are `FSB2`; a centre baked again or added in Europe takes `FS_CENTRES_HOLES=1`. v0.7.6's reader knows only `FSB1`, so the pushed code on the new ground needs the pushed `centres/` (decision 15).
- **The zone texture:** `euZoneData(header, values)` in `ground.js` makes a four-channel texture from the country raster (`data/europe/country.u8.gz`, loaded for the rules; one line in `geo-rules.js`, `countryValues`): R the UK and Ireland, G France and Monaco, B Spain, Portugal, Andorra and Gibraltar, A the Netherlands, each blurred by two 3-cell box passes (about 10 km). `terrain.js`'s `euZoneTexture()` binds it as `uEuZone` over `uEuZoneBox`, built once when the terrain is made (137 ms, 7.7 MB, behind the loading screen); `EU_ZONE_FALLBACK`, one texel, is v0.7.1's rule everywhere if the raster can't load. A country in none of the groups (`EU_ZONE_GROUPS`) keeps v0.7.1's rule.
- **`tools/bake_region_lakes.py`,** and why a later tile batch must run it on its new tiles: the tile bake (`compose_height()`) still draws inland water 2 m under its nearest bank, so a new tile's lakes come out as v0.7.6's did until this tool has run on it (and on the neighbours whose borders it changes). It reads the installed `r0` tiles and the batch's staged raw heights (`raw-h/<ti>_<tj>.npy`, as `bake_region_height.py` leaves them in the run's scratch: run it while they are still there), touches water cells only, never stores 0 in water, holds `LIFT_MAX` (5 m), seals each tile from its owners' cores, writes only into an empty staging folder (it refuses one that isn't), and its `--check <frozen dir>` proves every untouched file equal by SHA-256 and only water cells changed in the rest; installed by `tools/install-region.py`. Europe only (`FS_REGION=europe`).
- **`tools/bake_region_patches.py`:** `DEM_FLOOR` (a patch's floor, applied to the raw heights before the built-up filter and to the result: `{'schiphol': -7.0}`), `URBAN_KEEP` (boxes where the built-up filter is off, eased over 300 m: Lisbon's cliff at Cristo Rei) and `FS_PATCH_PLAIN=1` (neither mend: Schiphol's and Lisbon's patches come out bit-identical to the installed files, the proof that the sources still give them). - **The towns' edge blend** (`towns77Vert(src)`: eleven splices into Europe's branches of the cities' shader, each at an anchor that must occur once; `TOWNS77_GLSL` with `uCtr[4]`, `uCtrN`, `towns77Dist()`; `setCentreBoxes(fn)` from `world.js`): the four nearest centres' boxes within 20 km, listed again when the camera's block or the mask moves. ### Pitfalls found in v0.7.7

- **A finding measured before it is mended may not be what was logged,** as Cristo Rei's was: the 31 m was read 600 m south of the statue, whose ground was 87 m; the fault was the built-up filter pulling a cliff top towards the river, so the mend was an exemption from the filter, not a new patch. As v0.7.6's gates: measure first, at the place itself.
- **The elevation model already held the lakes' levels.** GLO-90 is hydro-flattened, and the batches' staged raw heights held every mapped lake flat at its level: no OSM `ele` tag and no download was needed. Look in what is already on disk before planning a source.
- **A finding at one place was general:** the IJsselmeer's 2 m was every lake's, and worst in the mountains (Loch Ness's surface climbing to 484 m). Measure across the region before scoping a mend.
- **The 250 m water mask joins a dam's foot to its reservoir,** so a rule that spreads a lake's level through the mask lifted the river below five dams by 8 to 31 m (the code review's first finding): check every moved cell against its own raw height, not only the lakes you meant.
- **Bake again from the pushed tiles:** before the lakes' second bake the 175 changed files were put back from the frozen copy and all 672 checked by SHA-256, so the tool read v0.7.6's tiles and not its own first output.
- **Prove the sources before the mend:** with `FS_PATCH_PLAIN=1` both patches came out bit-identical to the installed files, so what differs after is the mend alone.
- **Data with no switch can't be proved off against the pushed tree:** with both looks off, 15 of Europe's 26 views differed from the pushed v0.7.6, which is the new ground showing and can't be told from a leak. Serve the pushed code on the new data as a third root (`/base2/`, with the pushed `centres/` since v0.7.6's reader knows only `FSB1`) and prove the switches off against that.
- **A reviewer reads files as they stand at a time;** what changes after has no reviewer (`cities.js` after 23:05, `terrain.js` after 23:00). Note the times, and send the last changes for a second look, as this run did at 23:53: it found a quarter's sixth row never drawn and the plots' rows under the houses.
- **Build a doubtful round in a working copy** (`/tmp/flight-sim/v077/t3/`, served at `/t3/`), so the tree stays whole evidence for the round before; copy it in only on the reader's word, after checking the tree's files are still the backup's and the copy differs in exactly the files expected.
- **A look can be worse only from high up:** the gardens drawn by ground distance laid a lattice of green plots over Paris's suburbs from FL350; fade such detail by the true distance to the camera, and look at a far view of every near-view change.
- **A look the builder itself calls weak goes back for a second round behind its own switch** (the low suburbs, `?suburbs77=0`), so that it can be turned off alone without taking the rest of its part with it.
- **A brief's list can be wrong for a place:** "the British Isles" hedged takes in the Fens, which are open arable land. Follow it, and log the place.
- **A subagent's browser time runs out** (`towns` at about 42 of its 45 minutes): budget the pictures, and take the before and after in as few runs as possible.
- **v0.7.6's still hold:** a second mirror for OSM before the first download; the planning's count is not the rule's; a finding at four airports was the game's own at all of them; a fitted shape must clear every type; a picture can mislead both ways; and the older ones (a stalled download is not a missing file; OSM's runway way is not the runway; another agent's half-done work fails the suite; a frame-time check beside another browser means nothing; confirm a guard is in the file before the tool runs; keep a frozen copy of the pushed version before any edit; `--total` for every before and after; stop Chrome by PID only; check the commit's title after a push).

### What the code has today

- **One settings object for the whole flight** (`src/sim/weather.js`: `calmWeather()`, `normalizeWeather()`, `compileWeather()`, `windAt()`): the wind's true direction is set at the arrival (`refX`, `refY`) and turned to each place's north by the grid's convergence (`windTurnAt()`), so it is one wind everywhere. Everything is a function of position and time with a seed and no state: the live sim (1/120 s), the predicted path (1/30 s) and a jump's restore must read the same air. Whatever v0.6.5 does must keep that.
- **The presets and the METAR line** (`src/sim/weather-presets.js`): `presetWeather(id, ctx)`, `weatherContext(airports, plan)` (the arrival and every airport's place), a severe storm's microbursts placed near airports, `metarOf()` in each country's form (`kr`, `jp`, `cn`, `ru`, `kp`).
- **Where it is set:** New scenario's weather preset (`ui/setup.js`), the Weather tab (`ui/weather-window.js`, `applyWeather()` in `main.js`), `sim.setWeather()`; a rewind or a jump keeps the weather set now (`restore(snap, { keepWeather })`).
- **What reads it:** the sim (the air-relative velocity, the flat rating, the windshear detector, `state.altInd` and the BARO alert, the runway's state and hydroplaning), the autopilots (the track and the crab), the screens (wind readouts, BARO), the score's wind at touchdown and the logbook, the look (`world.setWeather()`: `sky.js`'s haze, `clouds.js`, `precip.js`, wet and snowy runways in `airports.js`), the sound (`synth.setWeather()`).

## Pitfalls already hit in the game

- **Golden flights recorded with one build of node can miss 1e-9 absolute on another** (v0.5.0): the Boeings' were recorded by the run with macOS's node, and Linux's (the Mac's VM, the cloud) flies them to roundoff only, the thrust in newtons then about 2e-9 apart. Compare to 1e-9 of the value above 1 (`goldenWorst()`); record new goldens where they will be checked, or compare the same way.
- **The cloud's upload folder keeps files staged by earlier sessions** (`/mnt/user-data/uploads/GameDump/...`): copy out only the files you just staged (by name), never the whole folder, or stale copies overwrite newer ones. Then compare the two trees' hashes.
- **A type's data must export every name `a321neo.js` does** (the data tests check). A missing one is `undefined`, and the first symptom is NaN everywhere.
- **Exit to menu saves.** A test that edits a save has to do it after that, or the edit is overwritten (it hid a v0.2.2-save check once).
- **`VMM_FACTOR` has to leave the amber band below Vref:** the law's default of 1.3 put it above Vref on every approach. Each Boeing sets its own.
- **Screenshots use the clock:** a check run at night is a night shot. `tests/browser-boeing-flight.mjs` fixes the afternoon.

- **The screens draw in B612,** whose digits are wider than Helvetica's, which the windows were first sized for. Text in a fixed window goes through `fitSize()` (pfd.js) or gets measured; check the PFD at FL350 and on final after any change (`tests/dev-ui.html` renders the screens without WebGL, so ordinary screenshots work there).
- **Runways that cross** have one surface: anything that needs a runway's height uses `profileAt()` or `rectElev()`, never a straight line between its ends, and `heightAt` (heightfield.js) and `flattenRunways` (glsl.js) must stay the same rule.
- **Coplanar pavement flickers.** Since v0.1.1 the pavement writes no depth and draws in a fixed order: taxilanes, then taxiways, then aprons and stands (see `apron.js`'s header). Anything new on the ground has to fit that scheme.
- **Flat triangles don't follow curving ground.** Pavement 8 cm up dipped under the terrain wherever a long triangle spanned a bend in it (v0.2.0: −0.58 m on Jeju's taxiway P). Anything new laid on the ground goes through `refineSoup()` (or `refineLine()`), and `tests/apron-ground.test.mjs` checks it on the real terrain.
- **Nothing lifted off the ground may overlap a runway.** The runway lies on its surface and wins by its pull towards the camera, which beats a few centimetres of lift only when seen from above; at the cockpit's grazing angles the lifted thing wins. That was v0.2.0's taxiway clipping. Clip ground pieces to `drawnRunwayRects()`, and put paint meant to show on a runway on its surface with `PULL.lead`.
- **The route's waypoints are the map's** (`map.waypoints`), not `plan.waypoints`, which is only what a flight started or loaded with. `captureState()` saves the map's.
- **While the A/THR drives the thrust lever, the sim writes `controls.thrust`** (v0.2.2). Anything else that sets it then is a pilot's move and disconnects the A/THR: a test or a tool that wants to move it should toggle the A/THR off first (as `tests/e2e-controller.js` does at the retard), or mean the takeover.
- **The engines' sound isn't steady on purpose** (v0.2.2): their tones waver and the two engines beat. `tests/audio-render.mjs`'s spool-up checks allow for that (1.5 dB a second, 2 dB in 300 ms); the fan tone is checked where it should be (18 × the shaft frequency), not as the strongest tone, which the buzz-saw can beat at high N1.
- **The fuselage isn't one ellipse per section any more.** Use `fuseSection()` and `fusePoint()`. After any change to the nose, check that the cockpit's interior stays inside the skin: v0.1.2's worst corner was at 0.993.
- **A runway end's x, y is its landing threshold:** the bake already applies `displacedM`. Applying it again put Jeju 31's glideslope 411 m down the runway until v0.1.3.
- **The thrust acts at `ENGINES`, not `NACELLES`.** Moving it changes the pitch moment from thrust and would need the sim recalibrated.
- **The scorer's track:**
  - a rewind calls `scorer.reset(true)`, which keeps the approach flown before it;
  - a new flight or a skip to final calls `reset()`;
  - a lift-off or a go-around clears it by itself.
- **Saves must stay deterministic.** `sim.snapshot()` round-trips through JSON exactly (`tests/scenario-state.test.mjs` flies a restored sim 120 s against the original, within 1e-6 m). New sim state that a step reads must go in the snapshot, or a loaded scenario will drift.
- **Night needs every ground shader:** new ground surfaces take `uSunColor`/`uSkyAmb` (the light), `uNight` (their own lights) and `aircraftLights()` (the lamp pools; `main.js` already scales the lamps by `uNight`, so don't multiply again).
- **Night-only objects hide by day** (`visible = false` under uNight 0.01): the day's draw calls must equal the day's before night existed.
- **Three windows share the Sky window's frame** (`.fssw`): the Weight window is `.fssw.fslw` and the Weather window (v0.4.0) `.fssw.fswx`. The Sky window is created first, so `.fssw [data-…]` still finds its parts first, but scope new selectors: the Sky window alone is `.fssw:not(.fslw):not(.fswx)` (v0.4.0's review had to fix `weight-copy.cjs` for it).
- **The A321's setup shows v0.1.0's empty weight and tanks** (50,100 kg and 18,864 kg, against the sim's 50,500 and 18,510). The Weight tab sums with the sim's (`loadNow()`); ROADMAP has it under "Seen while building v0.3.2".
- **The sim's touchdown g follows the sink rate** (about 1.3 g at 200 ft/min, 2.0 g at 360). That's why g weighs little in the score.
- **Calm is v0.3.2's arithmetic** (v0.4.0). The default weather is `null` in the sim, and every weather term is skipped or exactly zero there; `tests/golden-a321.test.mjs` checks it to 1e-9. New sim code has to keep that, and clear weather must keep drawing as before (weather-only objects hide in clear weather).
- **Nothing random in the sim:** the live sim steps at 1/120 s and the prediction at 1/30 s, and a jump restores the worker's snapshot, so anything that varies (the gusts, the turbulence, lightning) is a function of position and time, seeded, as `src/sim/weather.js` is.
- **`sim.weather` is replaced, never changed in place:** `main.js` redraws the world and tells the synth when its identity changes, and snapshots share the object.
- **The wing bend is a vertex-shader term after the skinning** (v0.4.3): anything that moves on a bone on the wing (slats, flaps, spoilers, the fan) is bent where it is, by `bendY`. A rigid part hung off the wing (an engine, a pylon) passes its attachment span as `bendY`, or the bend shears it and, on a turning part, travels round with it. The CPU's skinned positions (`getVertexPosition()`, the wing-gaps check) don't include the bend.
- **The dev pages don't spin the fans or bend the wings by themselves:** set `engines` (n1) and call `dev.flex(m)`, and wait between shots (`look.cjs`).
- **A canvas read must follow a draw in the same task** (v0.4.2): the renderer keeps no drawing buffer, so `toDataURL()` a frame later reads back black. Call `__fs.renderOnce()` in the same `evaluate` (`v042-shots.cjs`'s wiper frames).
- **`out` is a GLSL keyword:** a variable named `out` stops the shader compiling, and the error shows only in the console (v0.4.2's spray).
- **Check a visual fix on screen against the pushed version** before trusting a model test: v0.4.2's far-cloud change passed its own test and only blurred the clouds in every view a player sees.
- **The altimeter isn't the altitude** (v0.4.0): the autopilots, the PFDs and the FCU/MCP work in `state.altInd`, from the static pressure and the setting; the radio altimeter, the score, the map and the terrain use `state.pos.z`. An altitude set from geometry (a final start's ALT) has to go through the altimeter, or the AP climbs or descends to make it read right.
