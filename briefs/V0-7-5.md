### 5. Every runway end can be picked (Shane, 2026-11-22)

**Why 20 is missing.** v0.7.0's `ONE_WAY` table (`src/world/runways.js`) takes each AIP's one-way use as a block. An end used only for take-offs is left out of every landing list, and an end used only for landings out of every take-off list:
- `canLand()` and `canTakeOff()` filter New scenario's lists (`src/ui/setup.js`, about line 341);
- the map's Change the route filters the same way (`src/ui/map.js`, about lines 745 and 772);
- so do the start on a final, Skip to final and the test hook (`src/main.js`, about lines 410, 446, 678 and 713);
- `createAirports()` also clears such an end's ILS.

Barcelona's 20 is "Not available for landing" in Spain's AIP, so it was hidden.

**The Cowork session's check of all sixty airports** (2026-11-22, 03:30 KST): `ONE_WAY` as v0.7.3 pushed it, and every runway in the game against OurAirports.
- **Every open runway is in the game.** The only ones not baked are three grass strips: Charles de Gaulle's 08H/26H, Zurich's 01H/19H and Innsbruck's glider strip.
- **Ten ends can't be picked for landing,** all in Europe:
  - Frankfurt 18 (take-offs only) and 36 (not in use);
  - Madrid 14L, 14R, 36L and 36R (take-offs only);
  - Barcelona 20 (take-offs only);
  - Schiphol 36L and 18L (take-offs only, `[EST]`);
  - Hamburg 33 (take-offs only).
- **Ten can't be picked for take-off:**
  - Frankfurt 07L and 25R (landings only), and 36;
  - Madrid 32L, 32R, 18L and 18R (landings only);
  - Schiphol 18R and 36R (landings only, `[EST]`);
  - Hamburg 15 (landings only).

  Brussels 19 was the eleventh, the game's own limit; v0.7.4's fix 1 lifted it (`ONE_WAY.EBBR` is gone).
- **East Asia has none.** Its circling-only ends (Gimhae 18L and 18R, Wonsan 33L and 33R), like Innsbruck's 08, are offered already, as circling approaches.

**Make:**
- **Every end is offered for landing and for take-off,** in every list. That includes the six new airports' ends, whatever their AIPs say.
- **The AIP's use stays as information and as the default:**
  - the default runway is still an end that the AIP uses that way;
  - the ends it doesn't use that way are listed after those, with their note (for example "20 · take-offs only in the AIP"), as the standby runways are;
  - the map's "TAKE-OFF ONLY" and "NOT IN USE" labels become that note.
- **The beam:** v0.7.4's rule in `src/main.js` gives every end that is landed on straight in a 3° localizer and glideslope (the game always gave the Boeings one at every end). Make sure the newly offered ends get it too, so LOC and APPR work there. The ILS category shown stays the file's, and most of these ends show none.
- **A final and Skip to final** for every end that isn't circling-only.
- **What stays:** circling-only ends stay as they are (Innsbruck's 08 has terrain on its straight-in line).

**Check:**
- a landing on each of the ten, with its taxi-in to a stand (the airports' check's taxi-in mode);
- a take-off from each of the ten, by a stand's route. Where no stand has a route to its hold, use a start on the runway, and log which;
