## The parts, in priority order

### 1. The military bases in their own colour (Shane's item 1)

- **Today:** every airport's label is drawn in the map's cyan, and the destination's in magenta (`src/ui/map.js`, the airports' labels).
- **Make:**
  - **An airport's kind:** a field for it (`base` or `joint`) in the region's airport table (`src/world/regions.js`, where v0.7.6's bases are listed).
  - **The five pure bases** get a label colour of their own, distinct from the airports' cyan, the destination's magenta and the predicted path's lime. Amber is the first thought. The runways' lines take the same tint.
  - **The four joint airports** keep the cyan, with a small mark beside the name.
  - **The legend and the airport's tooltip** say "military base" or "joint civil and military".
- **Check:** the map in both regions with the bases in view, zoomed out and zoomed in.

### 2. The airports in full on the map (Shane's item 2)

**The data is in hand.** Each airport file has, besides the runways and terminals that the map draws today:
- `taxiways`, with `ref`, `kind`, `widthM` and `points`;
- `aprons`;
- `stands`, with `ref`;
- `holdingPositions`;
- the buildings.

**Make, as the map zooms in on an airport:**
- the aprons and the taxiways drawn as pavement at their widths, under the runways;
- the taxiways' names along them, spaced so that they don't crowd;
- the hold-short bars, and the runways' numbers at their ends;
- the stands' numbers once zoomed right in;
- the terminals, as v0.7.4 drew them.

Set each layer's zoom so that the airport reads in full without clutter. Keep the map's frame time inside its budget, with each airport's shapes built once and cached.

**The assigned taxi route on the map:** while taxiing, the route the game gave you is drawn as a line from the aircraft to its end (the hold, or the stand), in the route's colour, and cleared when you reach it.

**Check:**
- stills of six airports zoomed in: Incheon, Haneda, Heathrow, Charles de Gaulle, Frankfurt and a base;
- a taxi with the route drawn;
- the map's frame time zoomed in, on mains and alone.

### 3. Cities at night (Shane's item 3)

**Today** (`src/world/citylights.js`): point sprites on a generic block grid (400 m blocks, rings to 128 km), switched on wherever the built-up cover says so. That is why a city from above reads as random specks.

**What makes a city read from above at night is its roads.** Make:
- **The main roads lit.** OSM's motorways, trunks and primary roads, baked into a small file per region (gzip; log the sizes, aiming under about 10 MB a region):
  - inside the cities, from the extracts already in `reference/raw/osm/`;
  - between them, from Natural Earth's roads (public domain) or a download.

  Streetlights run along them as continuous lines, brighter on the bigger roads.
- **Cars on the motorways:** white headlights one way and red tail lights the other, moving along the carriageways, thinning late at night, and repeating in time so that a jump or the predicted path is unaffected. They are a look only.
- **The city's shape:**
  - downtowns brighter and denser;
  - parks, rivers, rail yards and water dark;
  - the residential fill irregular, not a grid, between the lit roads.
- **The colours:** warmer sodium orange in Europe's older streets, whiter LED in Korea's and Japan's cities, a mix in China's. A seeded variety within each city.
- **Behind `?lights79=0`,** which gives v0.7.8's lights back.

**Check:**
- Seoul, Tokyo, Shanghai, Paris, London and Frankfurt by night, from 3,000 ft and FL100, before and after;
- a motorway's cars close up;
- 60 fps by night over Tokyo and Paris, on mains and alone.

### 5. The gates' leftovers ("Found while building v0.7.8")

- **Each type's stop bar:** the bar is the A321's at every gate, so the 787's and 777's nose wheels stop 1 to 2 m off it. Give each type its own stop mark, or park each with its nose wheel on the bar.
- **The gates with no bridge:** 181 of the 2,480 have none (Qingdao lost 13 with the fit). Look at why at each airport, and give a bridge wherever a wall and the nose's clearance allow.
- **Two bridges at Heathrow** end in open apron. Mend them.
- **Fairford's hardstands** stand about 38 m from the taxiway. Join them with pavement.
- **Check:** a gate with each type at Heathrow, Qingdao and one more, and Fairford from above.

### 7. Cities by day, past 8 km ("Found while building v0.7.7")

Beyond about 8 km, the towns read as flat grey ground. Give the far towns a texture of their own:
- blocks and streets in the ground's colour by the built-up cover;
- the real centres' footprints as a darker mass;
- parks and rivers.

Match it with the near town at the hand-over. Check from 3,000 ft and FL100 over London, Paris, Seoul and Tokyo, by day.

### 8. The looks still weak ("Found while building v0.7.8" and earlier)

- **The Rock of Gibraltar is still soft:** GLO-30 holds 369.7 m of its 426. Use a finer public source if one covers it and its licence fits a public repository; else shape the ridge from its published heights, `[EST]`.
- **Notre-Dame de la Garde's hill** is a smooth dome with the streets draped on it. Give it its steep limestone sides.
- **The Bergisel jump's in-run** is a thin strip over the trees. Give it its depth.
- **The park trees** are simple cones and faceted mounds. Give the broadleaf trees round crowns.
- **Barcelona's harbour basin** is drawn darker than the sea, with a straight edge where the coast patch ends.
- **Cheongju's centre** needs a newer Korea extract (a download), so the downtown is real.
- **Kanazawa's centre** can't be told from its town at 3,000 ft.

### 9. Weather by place's second half (v0.7.4 left it as later work)
