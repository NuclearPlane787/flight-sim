2. **The military sides are empty:** no parked aircraft and no stands there. Zaragoza's civil apron gets airliners as usual.
3. **The stop:** ready to push by 22:00 KST.

## The Cowork session's choices (told to him; his to change)

- **Ramstein isn't classified in what the game uses.** OSM maps its runways, taxiways and buildings in public. Anything a published source doesn't give is `[EST]`, as at every other airport.

  - it also offers a few spots on a ramp for the player alone (OSM's parking positions, or placed by the bake and marked `[EST]`, as Innsbruck's are);
