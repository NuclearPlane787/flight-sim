### 3. The rules

- **Gibraltar:**
  - its zone, `Europe/Gibraltar`;
  - its country code, its own or the UK's with the zone;
  - hPa and feet;
  - its transition altitude from its data, else `[EST]`.
- **Innsbruck, Porto, Hamburg, Marseille and Lyon:** each airport's own transition altitude where its AD 2.17 sets one. Austria's and Portugal's were read in v0.7.1.
- **Venice and Naples:** `[EST]` if Italy's AIP refuses.
- **Morocco:** check its rules once more now that its north is at 250 m (`Africa/Casablanca`).

### 4. The centres and the landmarks

- **Real centres for:** Hamburg, Venice, Marseille, Lyon, Porto and Naples, boxed tightly. Venice's old city is all small buildings on islands: its look from 3,000 ft is the point.
- **Landmarks,** as our own models from public figures, with no logos or names, in this order:
  - Hamburg's Elbphilharmonie (about 110 m) and Heinrich-Hertz-Turm (about 279 m);
  - St Mark's Campanile in Venice (about 99 m);
  - Marseille's Notre-Dame de la Garde on its hill above the port;
  - Lyon's Fourvière basilica on its hill;
  - Porto's Dom Luís I Bridge, the iron arch over the Douro;
  - Innsbruck's Bergisel ski jump on its hill.

  Vesuvius and the Rock are the terrain itself: check them from 3,000 ft and from the finals.
