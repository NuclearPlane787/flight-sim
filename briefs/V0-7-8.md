## The parts, in priority order

### 1. The gates at every airport ("Found while building v0.7.6")

- **The finding:** at every airport, the stop bar (`NOSE_WHEEL`, 15.7 m ahead of the stand point) and the jet bridge's door (`DOOR_FWD`, 17 m ahead) stand 21 m ahead of the aircraft they serve.
  - `apron-geom.js` (v0.1.0) lays them out for an aircraft whose reference point is on the stand point.
  - `gate-flow.js` and `traffic-layout.js` park every aircraft with its nose on it.
- **What exists:** v0.7.6 fitted four airports (`GATE_FIT`: Centrair, Itami, Hiroshima, Cheongju). `?gatefit=1` (node `FS_GATEFIT=1`) lays every airport out so.
- **Make:**
  - the fit at every airport, by default;
  - the bridge's cab at each type's own front door: the widebodies' doors lie further back than the A321's, 4 m behind the nose;
  - the lead-in line ending at the stop bar, not 5.3 m past it;
  - the gate pairs' stills camera close enough to show the stop bar.
- **Check:**
  - a gate at Incheon, Haneda, Heathrow, Charles de Gaulle, Frankfurt and Barcelona, each with an A321 and a 777;
  - the pushbacks' checks again at three of them (the airports' check's day run).

### 2. Smooth the crossings ("Found while building v0.7.4")

- **The finding:** where runways cross at an angle, the surfaces step by up to 33 cm at 20 m off the centreline, at Dublin, Prague, Cologne Bonn and Jeju.
- **Make:** each such crossing gets the bends that v0.7.4 gave Brussels's (four per crossing), so that no step over 2 cm is left anywhere on either runway's pavement. Shane accepted that this changes ground already flown.
- **Check:**
  - the step at every crossing in the game, logged before and after;
  - the four airports' day runs, and a landing over each crossing;
  - `tests/v072-eu-crossings.test.mjs` with every runway;
  - East Asia's Jeju views: they change, so log them.

### 3. Landmarks and bases

From "Found while building v0.7.3" to "… v0.7.6".

**The landmarks** (our own models from public figures, with no logos or names):
- **Innsbruck's Bergisel jump** reads as a small tower at the slope's foot. Draw the inrun down the hill and the tower at its top.
- **Hamburg's Elbphilharmonie** is coarse. It needs its wave-shaped glass top on the brick warehouse.
- **Notre-Dame de la Garde** has no hill under it (56 m in the 250 m grid). Give it a 30 m patch, so that it stands on its 150 m hill.
- **The Rock of Gibraltar** looks low and soft from the finals' distance. Make its ridge sharp in the patch, at its real height of about 426 m.
- **Tower Bridge's south end** stands in the river (v0.7.4). Put it on its bank.
- **The castles' colours** at Nagoya and Osaka read pale mint and pale grey, because the shader lifts them. Give the landmarks their own colours.

**The bases:**
- **The shelters** are drawn as 9 m boxes. Draw them as low vaults.
- **Hangars and pads** stand unjoined to any taxiway (Zaragoza's military side, a pad at Lossiemouth). Join them with pavement.
- **Fairford's apron edges** are stepped. Smooth them.
- **Zaragoza's stands 1 and 2** overlap, as OSM maps them. Keep one.

**Check:** each landmark's view from above and level, before and after, and each base from above.

### 4. Trees and towers ("Found while building v0.7.5")

- **The findings:**
  - trees stand through a building in Hiroshima's Peace Park;
  - a conifer forest surrounds the Atomic Bomb Dome and both castles;
  - in both cases, the ground cover's tree class sits under a park, with nothing keeping trees off the centres' buildings;
  - generated towers stand in a wall just beyond Itami's fence, and tall dark blocks stand under Kunsan's final to 18. That is the cities' generator on dense built-up cells.
- **Make:**
  - no tree on a real building's footprint or a landmark's, and parks in the centres drawn as parkland (lawns and scattered trees), not forest;
  - the generator keeps its tall blocks out of an airport's boundary and its first kilometres of final: low buildings there, as real airports' surroundings are;
  - a real mapped area wins over the generator where OSM has one (Kunsan's industrial complex).
- **Check:**
  - Hiroshima's Peace Park and the Dome;
  - both castles;
  - Itami's 32L final over the fence;
  - Kunsan's final to 18;
  - three of Europe's centres, to show nothing else moved.
