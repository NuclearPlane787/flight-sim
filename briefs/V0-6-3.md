## The rules every part keeps

`briefs/V0-6-0.md`'s, and these:

- **Nothing already flown moves.** No existing tile's core cells change (heights, water, cover, fine cover): only the 2-cell borders of tiles that gain a neighbour, and the 1 km tiles that now average 250 m data. No existing airport file, patch or coast patch changes. The three golden flights to 1e-9; the 1237 unit tests keep their assertions, except where a test counts airports, patches, runways or tiles, or pins `FINE_EXTENT`.
- **The flags stay** (`EAST_ASIA_ON`, `CITIES_ON`, `GROUND_ON`): `?eastasia=0&cities=0` is still v0.5.8's Korea pixel for pixel. Their removal is v0.7.0's question.
- **A new airport waits behind `PENDING_ON`** (`?pending=1`, `FS_PENDING=1`; the manifest's `airportsPending`, `patchesPending`) until it passes the check every airport passed: a gate start, pushback, taxi, take-off and a landing by the test pilot, and taxi-in from each end; the gate and a final by day and by night; 60 fps at a stand and on a 3 nm final. Then its entries move to `airportsRegion` and `patchesRegion`. The game stays pushable at every checkpoint.
- **The bake:** every step through the guard (2 at once, 20 minutes, 6 GB), tile by tile, resumable, into staging (`/tmp/flight-sim/v063/`), never straight into `data/`. v0.6.0's staging (`/tmp/flight-sim/v060/bake/`: the raw heights, water, counts and cover of all 180 old tiles) is still on disk and is what the new tiles' borders read. Bake each tile once.
- **The sources:** GLO-90 for 24–46° N is on disk; WorldCover's `N36E114`, `N39E114`, `N42E120` to `N42E132` and seven GLO-30 tiles are downloading (`/tmp/flight-sim/v063/queue.tsv`, `downloads.log`); `china-261118.osm.pbf` is on disk (Beijing, Tianjin, Shenyang, Dalian) and Geofabrik's `far-eastern-fed-district-261119.osm.pbf` is downloading (Vladivostok). China's AIP can't be read: open secondary sources, marked as such, else `[EST]`. Nothing of any source goes in the repository (`reference/` is git-ignored).
- **Public repository:** no third-party documents, images or models; no absolute paths with the Mac's user name; no real airline's branding, no logos or names on buildings.
- **Never run git in GameDump.** Stay in `games/flight-sim/`, pictures in `Claude outputs/flight-sim/` (`v063-*.png`), scratch in `/tmp/flight-sim/v063/`. The session's own no-cache server on 8014 (`tests/cloud/serve.py`), never 8001. Playwright is in `/tmp/flight-sim/pw/`.
- **Frame rates only on mains and alone on the GPU** (`pmset -g batt` first: on battery under 20 % Chrome halves the frame rate).
