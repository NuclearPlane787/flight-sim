## Europe: its frame, its shape, its size

**The frame.** Europe gets its own Lambert conformal conic on WGS84, built as `frame.js`'s:

- origin at **47° N 4° E**;
- standard parallels at **39° and 54°** (n ≈ 0.7275);
- scale: +0.74 % at Gibraltar (36.15° N), −0.85 % at 47° N, −0.13 % at Dublin, +0.54 % at Edinburgh, and +1.7 % at the region's northern edge (59° N, where there is only 1 km land);
- grid convergence up to about ±11° at the east and west edges (Lisbon −9.6°, Vienna +9.2°).

A check that compares a baked length with a published one divides by the scale there first.

**The lattice.** Its origin is the frame's origin (`x0 = y0 = 0`: tile `0_0`'s south-west corner is at 47° N 4° E). Everything else is as East Asia's:

- the 250 m level in 128 km tiles (512 cells, 2-cell borders);
- the 1 km level in 512 km tiles;
- the 31 m cover in 32 km sub-files (`perTile` 4).

**The region's outline** is a polygon in latitude and longitude, with edges along the parallels and meridians between its points (densified every 0.25°). It bounds the 1 km land, the map's edge and where the predicted path ends. Its points:

- 35.0° N 11.0° W, 59.0° N 11.0° W, 59.0° N 19.5° E;
- 42.5° N 19.5° E, 39.6° N 18.6° E;
- 36.3° N 16.0° E, 36.3° N 12.0° E, 37.6° N 11.0° E;
- 37.6° N 1.0° E, 35.0° N 2.5° W.

What that gives:

- **The north at 59° N** takes in all of mainland Scotland, Denmark and Gothenburg.
- **The east at 19.5° E** gives Vienna about 220 km of room and puts Budapest at the edge.
- **The south-east cut** leaves out Algeria and Tunisia but keeps Sicily.
- **The south-west** keeps Morocco's coast along the Strait, so Gibraltar will look across at Africa.

**The 1 km level** covers the region's whole bounding rectangle from v0.7.0 on: 34 tiles of 512 km, 30 with land, about 8 MB.

**The 250 m data** is every 128 km tile with land within **200 km of an airport's reference point**: about 215 tiles once all 37 are in. It comes in batches.

## Pitfalls known before you start

- **Western longitudes.** Heathrow, Gatwick and Madrid are west of Greenwich, and East Asia was all east of it. The source tiles' names use `W` there: Copernicus `…_N51_00_W001_00_DEM`, and WorldCover `N51W003`. Check every name builder and every `lon > 0` assumption, and test a tile on each side of 0°.
- **Land below sea level.**
  - Schiphol is about 3 m below sea level, among polders down to about −6 m.
  - `bake_geom.py`'s `compose_height()` lifts all land to at least 0.5 m (line ~288), which would leave Schiphol's flattened field in a pit.
  - Let land stay below 0 m where the land polygons say land, and check that the shader, the sim and the water treat land below 0 m as land.
- **Summer time.** Europe changes its clocks (the last Sundays of March and October), and none of East Asia's zones did.
  - No fixed offsets anywhere.
  - Zone labels follow the date: GMT/BST, WET/WEST, CET/CEST.
  - Gibraltar keeps Central European time, not London's.
  - Morocco is `Africa/Casablanca`.
  - `ICAO_ZONES` in `clock.js` gains Europe's prefixes.
- **Transition altitudes:** the United Kingdom, Spain and Italy set them airport by airport (each AIP's AD 2.17); most others nationally (ENR 1.7). `COUNTRY_RULES` needs a per-airport transition that applies near that airport, with the country's elsewhere.
- **One-way runways.**
  - Frankfurt's 18 is take-off only (southward) and 07L/25R is landing only.
  - Gatwick's northern runway (08L/26R) is a standby, used only when the main one (08R/26L) is shut; routine use is expected from 2030. List it, but never pick it by default.
  - Add a table like `CIRCLING_ONLY` for runways used one way, from each AIP.
- **The check chain runs 37 times over Update 7,** so make it a script in the repository: the one-airport chain and the promotion, from v0.6.4's `/tmp/flight-sim/v064/ap.sh` and `promote.py`, without user paths. If `/tmp` was cleared by a reboot, rebuild them from v0.6.4's `STATUS.md` entries.
